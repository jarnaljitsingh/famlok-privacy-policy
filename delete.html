How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account directly from the app or by contacting us:

Option 1: Delete from the App

  1. Open the Famlok app
  2. Tap on your Profile Photo (top left)
  3. Go to Profile Settings
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Option 2: Request via Email

Send us an email with the subject "Delete My Account" and include the email address associated with your Famlok account:

Email: support@famlok.online

We will process your request within 3 business days.

What Data Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, email, photo)
  • Your location history
  • Your circle memberships
  • Your saved places and geofence data
  • Your driving history and events
  • All associated alerts and notifications
Please note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You will be removed from all circles. If you are the admin of a circle, the circle will be deleted for all members.

Data Retention

After account deletion, all your personal data is removed immediately from our active systems. Some data may be retained in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before being permanently purged.
