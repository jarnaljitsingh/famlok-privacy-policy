Famlok Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026  ·  Applies to the Famlok app on Apple iOS and Google Android.

This Privacy Policy explains how Famlok (“Famlok”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, holds and discloses your personal information when you use the Famlok mobile application and related services (the “App”).

Famlok is a family location-sharing and safety app offered to residents of Australia. We handle your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Please read this policy carefully, because the App collects precise, real-time location and other sensitive information and shares it with the people in your family circle.

The short version. Famlok shares your live location, places, drives and safety alerts with members of your family circle. To do this we collect precise location continuously and in the background — even when the App is closed. We store your data using Google Firebase (in the United States), and we send a coarse area code (your postcode/suburb) to an AI provider (Moonshot AI / Kimi, in China) to estimate neighbourhood safety. You can see, correct or delete your data at any time, and you can delete your account from inside the App.

1. Who we are

Famlok is operated by its independent developer (a sole operator) (“we”). We are responsible for the personal information collected through the App. You can contact us using the details in section 13.

2. What personal information we collect

We collect the following categories of personal information. We only collect what we need to run the features you use.

2.1 Account & identity

  • Your name, email address and profile photo (provided when you sign in with Google, or that you add yourself);
  • A phone number, if you choose to add one;
  • A user account identifier and a randomly-generated device identifier (used to enforce single-device login for your security);
  • A push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android; Apple Push Notification service on iOS) so we can send you alerts.

2.2 Precise location — collected in the background

Important: When you grant location permission, Famlok collects your precise location continuously, including in the background and when the App is closed, and resumes automatically after your device restarts. This is necessary for live family sharing, place arrival/departure alerts and drive detection. You can pause sharing in the App, change permissions in your device settings, or uninstall the App at any time.
  • GPS and network-based location (latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed and heading);
  • Your device’s battery level (shown to your family so they know if your phone is running low);
  • A street address derived from your coordinates (reverse-geocoded for display);
  • Location is updated frequently (roughly every 30 seconds in the background, and more often when the map is open).

2.3 Motion and activity

  • Activity type detected by your device (for example: still, walking, or in a vehicle);
  • Motion-sensor data (accelerometer/related sensors) used to detect when a drive starts and to recognise driving events. This is used to provide drive features and to save battery — we do not use it for health or fitness profiling.

2.4 Driving information

  • Drive routes and route points recorded during a trip;
  • Driving events such as speeding, hard braking, rapid acceleration and phone handling while driving;
  • Drive summaries and scores.

Drive scores are automated estimates produced for family awareness only.

2.5 Places and geofence history

  • Places you save (for example Home, School or Work), including their coordinates and alert radius;
  • Geofence events — a record of when you arrive at or leave a saved place — and how long you have been there.

2.6 Safety, SOS and messages

  • SOS / emergency alerts you send, including your location at that time;
  • “No-show” and other safety alerts;
  • Quick messages you send to family members.

2.7 Neighbourhood safety estimates

  • To estimate the safety of an area, we send a coarse area identifier (your postcode / locality) — not your name or precise coordinates — to our AI provider, and store the resulting safety score and any related local-news items.

2.8 Subscription & billing

  • Your subscription status, product identifier, purchase/transaction token and expiry/trial dates.
  • We do not receive or store your credit-card or payment-card details — all payments are processed by Apple or Google.

2.9 Technical, diagnostic & support information

  • Crash reports and analytics events (to keep the App working and to improve it);
  • Diagnostic logs stored on your device, which may include information such as postcodes, geofence events and drive activity;
  • If you submit a bug report, a screenshot, device information and diagnostic logs you choose to attach.

4. Who we share your information with

4.1 Your family circle

The core purpose of Famlok is to share information with the people in your circle. When you join a circle, other members can see your real-time and historical location, your places, your drives, your battery level, your safety status and your messages. Only share your location with people you trust, and only join circles run by people you trust.

We may disclose personal information where required or authorised by law, to protect someone’s safety, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the App (in which case this policy will continue to apply).

5. Overseas disclosure (sending data outside Australia)

As shown in the table above, your personal information is stored and processed outside Australia, including in the United States (Google/Firebase, Apple, Cloudflare, Cloudinary, Mapbox) and China (Moonshot AI / Kimi, which receives only a coarse area code).

By using Famlok and providing your personal information, you consent to the disclosure of your personal information to these overseas recipients. You acknowledge that, because you have consented to these overseas disclosures, Australian Privacy Principle 8.1 will not apply to them, and that we will not be accountable under the Privacy Act for how those overseas recipients handle your personal information. Overseas recipients may be required to disclose information under the laws of their own country, which differ from Australian privacy law.

6. Children and young people

Famlok is a family app and is designed to be used by families, including children, with parental involvement.

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Famlok.
  • If you are under 15, a parent or guardian must set up your participation and consent to it on your behalf, and you should use Famlok on a device your parent or guardian manages.
  • We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create their own account.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us (section 13) and we will delete it.

We monitor developments in Australian children’s privacy law (including the forthcoming Children’s Online Privacy Code) and will update our practices as required.

8. How we protect your information

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
  • Access to your stored data is restricted by server-side security rules so that, generally, only you and the members of your circle can access your data.
  • We enforce single-device login to reduce the risk of unauthorised access.
  • On your device, your device identifier is stored in the secure keychain on iOS. Some information (such as cached subscription status, your area postcode, and diagnostic logs) is stored in ordinary app storage and is not encrypted; for that reason we recommend you protect your phone with a passcode or biometric lock.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Information stored on your device

To work offline and start quickly, the App stores some information locally on your device, including your last-known status, cached subscription status, your area postcode, your device identifier, and diagnostic logs. When you delete your account, the App clears these local caches and your device identifier from your device.

10. Your rights — access, correction and complaints

  • Access & correction: you can view and update much of your information directly in the App. You may also ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or to correct it.
  • Delete your account: in the App, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and associated data from our systems (see section 7 for the profile-photo exception). Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Stop sharing: you can pause location sharing, leave a circle, or revoke permissions in your device settings at any time.

Making a complaint. If you have a privacy concern, contact us first (section 13) and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au, phone 1300 363 992.

11. Data breaches

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the OAIC as required by the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under the Privacy Act.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and change the “Last updated” date. Significant changes will be notified to you in the App or by other reasonable means. Your continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.
